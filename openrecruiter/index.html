// WHAT IS OpenRecruiter

Recruitment AI for
small teams & indie recruiters

Built for independent recruiters and small teams working across industries they don't have deep expertise in. Upload a job description and candidate resumes — OpenRecruiter does the heavy lifting.

📄

Upload & Parse

Supports PDF, DOCX, and TXT. Paste a JD, drop in resumes, and go.

⚡

Candidate Scoring

AI scores each candidate's fit against the job description instantly.

🔍

Skill Gap Analysis

Identifies exactly where a candidate falls short — no guesswork.

✉️

Outreach Emails

Generates personalized outreach emails for each shortlisted candidate.

🗂️

Kanban Pipeline

Drag-and-drop board to track every candidate through your pipeline.

🔒

100% Local

Runs entirely on your machine. Your data never leaves your device.

RecruitGPT

The brain behind the matching.

RecruitGPT is the open-source post-training pipeline that powers OpenRecruiter's matching intelligence. While OpenRecruiter handles the interface and workflow, RecruitGPT trains the specialized models that make candidate-job matching truly accurate.
